Zwolle Support

Zwolle rides structured cycling workouts on your indoor trainer, from Intervals.icu or .ZWO files, on Android, Windows, iPhone and iPad.

Get in touch

For questions, problems or ideas, email lle-support@proton.me.

If something isn't working, it helps to include:

  • your phone, tablet or PC, and its system version
  • Zwolle's version, shown at the bottom of Settings
  • your trainer, power meter and other sensors
  • what you did, and what happened

What you need

A Bluetooth power meter on any indoor trainer, or a smart trainer that reports power. On a smart trainer (FTMS, or older Tacx trainers through Tacx's own Bluetooth protocol), Zwolle can also hold your target power for you in ERG mode. Heart rate monitors and cadence sensors are optional.

Privacy

Zwolle has no account of its own, no ads and no tracking. See the privacy policy.
